# Provider inventory review — 2026-09-20

Reviewed all 46 registered provider keys. Updated 36 inventories: 452 provider-location memberships added and 258 removed. The generated catalog changes from 452 to 505 location keys (74 added, 21 removed). All provider keys remain available; confirmed retired services have empty arrays.

Public sources were checked on 2026-09-20. A listed region with temporary outages, limited capacity, or closed new sales can still be active. Announcements alone do not establish an active site, and announced retirement alone does not prove a completed shutdown. Inventories use airport/metro coordinates rather than facility coordinates. Counts below are normalized codes, not physical server-site counts.

Some providers do not publish a complete current city list. The coverage column calls out partial reconciliations, conflicting sources, and inferred geography; a completed source review does not certify those inventories as exhaustive.
